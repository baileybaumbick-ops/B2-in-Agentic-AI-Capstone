# Quarterly recommendation: prioritize billing/support (address free‑tier playback restrictions first)

**Recommended area:** `billing_support`

Recommendation

Prioritize the billing/support area this quarter, with the immediate focus on (1) Free tier playback and skip restrictions and (2) related messaging/FAQ/support flows that explain limits to free users. The issue billing.free_tier_restrictions has the highest baseline priority_score at 25 [C04], so it should be the first engineering and comms effort this quarter. A secondary, short workstream should coordinate with usability to monitor ads/controls impact on perceived value (see Alternatives below).

Evidence

- The top-ranked issue by our baseline priority (complaint_count x mean_severity = severity_sum) is Free tier playback and skip restrictions: complaint_count 9 [C01], severity_sum 25 [C02], mean_severity 2.777778 [C03], priority_score 25 [C04], cancellation_intent_members 1 [C05].
- Billing/support area totals align with that concentration: complaints 9 [A3COM], severity_sum 25 [A3SEV], mean_severity 2.777778 [A3MEA].
- Representative user comments (examples): `d146397f-4a94-4980-be00-ae9212eca3a7` ("you can't even pick a certain song without Spotify premium"), `89a8e180-7846-4421-a2b6-fa7787e9d0d2` ("Too much restrictions"), `88439ec2-7c49-4c57-bedb-c5e098692aff` ("I would request to remove all the new features .").

Alternatives considered

- Usability: the usability area has complaints 13 [A2COM] and severity_sum 31 [A2SEV] with mean_severity 2.384615 [A2MEA]. Its top sub-issues include Excessive and intrusive ads (complaint_count 7 [C11], priority_score 15 [C14]) and Missing/worsened playback controls (complaint_count 4 [C16], priority_score 11 [C19]). These are important but their top issue priority_score 15 [C14] is below the billing top issue priority_score 25 [C04], so they are recommended as secondary or coordinated work.
- Playback: playback area shows complaints 6 [A4COM], severity_sum 21 [A4SEV], mean_severity 3.500000 [A4MEA]; the crash/freeze issue has complaint_count 2 [C26] and priority_score 8 [C29]. Playback stability matters (and has higher mean severity) but currently ranks lower by baseline priority than the billing free‑tier issue.
- Access: access area has complaints 2 [A5COM], severity_sum 8 [A5SEV], mean_severity 4.000000 [A5MEA]; the login_failure issue has complaint_count 2 [C21] and priority_score 8 [C24]. Access problems are high-severity for affected users but are smaller in volume in this sample than the billing free‑tier complaints.

Risks and limitations

- Data are self-selected Google Play reviews (May 2022–Nov 2023); not a customer population sample. The method notes state cancellation language is expressed intent, not observed churn.
- Coverage shows source_rows 100 [K01] and completed_records 100 [K02]; quarantined/pending are 0 [K03][K04][K05]. Verifier agreement and label quality are imperfect (see method notes). These limitations mean priority ordering could shift if we observed: (a) measured cancellations or support ticket volume tied to billing changes, (b) broader telemetry showing widespread login or crash failures, or (c) a different distribution in other app stores or support channels.

What would change this recommendation

- If product telemetry or support tickets show materially higher actual cancellations or assists attributable to playback restrictions than reflected here, escalate to a cross‑functional fix and reallocate resources accordingly. If instead crashes or access failures surface at scale in telemetry, shift to playback or access work.
