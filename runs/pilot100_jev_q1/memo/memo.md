# Prioritize billing/support: fix premium-only playback restrictions first

**Recommended area:** `billing_support`

Recommendation

Prioritize the billing/support area this quarter, focusing first on the Premium-only playback restrictions issue (billing.free_tier_restrictions). Tackle (1) the restrictions that prevent selecting specific songs and limit skips, and (2) the related communication/FAQ or in-app messaging that explains free-tier behavior. As secondary, coordinate with usability on ad frequency and basic playback controls (usability.ads, usability.controls) to address complaints that frame restrictions as part of a broader “forced upgrade” experience.

Evidence

- Highest single-issue priority (severity_sum) in the corpus is Premium-only playback restrictions with severity_sum 25 [C02] (priority_score 25 [C04]) and complaint_count 9 [C01]; there is also recorded cancellation language of 1 member for this issue [C05].
- Billing/support area totals: complaints 9 [A3COM], severity_sum 25 [A3SEV], mean_severity 2.777778 [A3MEA].
- Usability topics that amplify the anger around billing restrictions: Excessive or disruptive ads has complaint_count 7 [C11] and severity_sum 14 [C12] (priority_score 14 [C14]); Playback control and feature loss has complaint_count 4 [C16] and severity_sum 11 [C17] (priority_score 11 [C19]).

- Ads pressure toward premium: `e5e6c55f-3069-4b7c-9807-f8b5d5bf2726` ("Too many ads...."), `3e8bdc92-4934-457e-85cc-8b9d14bf51c0` ("always every 1 or 2 song then instant ads").

Alternatives considered

- Usability: the usability area has a larger cumulative severity (severity_sum 30 [A2SEV]) and more complaints (complaints 13 [A2COM]), but no single usability sub-issue reaches the top individual priority score. The highest usability sub-issue is Excessive ads (severity_sum 14 [C12]), which amplifies frustration but ranks below the billing.free_tier_restrictions item by baseline priority.
- Playback: playback area has severity_sum 21 [A4SEV] and a high mean_severity 3.500000 [A4MEA], with crash/freeze reports (severity_sum 8 [C27]) that are severe per instance but lower in aggregate than the billing issue. These technical fixes are important but are not the single highest-priority complaint by baseline score.
- Access: access area shows mean_severity 4.000000 [A5MEA] for a small set of complaints (severity_sum 8 [A5SEV]), but the volume is low (complaints 2 [A5COM]), so it ranks lower by our baseline priority rule.

Risks and limitations

- Data are self-selected Google Play reviews (see method limitations); these are not a representative customer sample and do not measure actual churn or revenue impact.
- Baseline priority uses complaint_count x mean_severity (severity_sum) as the primary ranking; that gives heavy weight to the single top billing issue (25 [C02]/25 [C04]) even though usability has larger cumulative severity (30 [A2SEV]). If leadership prefers maximizing total severity reduced each quarter, usability might instead be prioritized.
- Coverage metadata: source_rows 100 [K01], completed_records 100 [K02], quarantined_records 0 [K03], pending_records 0 [K04], empty_text_quarantines 0 [K05].

What would change the recommendation

- If we observe widening cancellation behavior tied to ads or restrictions (higher cancellation counts), or quantitative signals from instrumentation showing large-scale upgrade conversions tied to restrictions, shift effort toward the area with the larger net impact. If product telemetry shows many crashes at scale, prioritize playback engineering instead.
