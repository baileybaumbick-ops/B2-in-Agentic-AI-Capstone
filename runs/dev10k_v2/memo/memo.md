# Prioritize billing & free-tier playback restrictions

**Recommended area:** `billing_support`

Recommendation

Prioritize billing_support work this quarter, focusing first on the Free tier paywall and skip limits issue (billing.free_tier_restrictions) and adjacent billing/support flows that amplify user anger and cancellation intent. Fixes should include restoring expected basic playback behaviors for free users where feasible, clearer in-app messaging about limitations, and faster/responsive billing/support resolution paths tied to free/premium feature boundaries.

Evidence

- The top issue by baseline priority is Free tier paywall and skip limits: priority_score 2,654 [C04], complaint_count 920 [C01], severity_sum 2,654 [C02], mean_severity 2.884783 [C03], with reported cancellation intent members 190 [C05]. Representative reviewer excerpts: `628ffdd2-7107-4617-84f6-63140c6ee91c` ("being denied to play a song"), `0d28f052-7d14-4c47-a5d9-9188b1663103` ("Problem with specific song selection"), `f36cd96b-90da-4fd9-95b3-0ef85c3a7896` ("Every single feature now requires you to purchase premium.").

- Billing/support area totals show concentrated volume and severity: complaints 1,036 [A2COM], severity_sum 2,978 [A2SEV], cancellations 203 [A2CAN], mean_severity 2.874517 [A2MEA]. These area-level aggregates indicate billing/support has the highest area-level severity among the four mapped areas.

Alternatives considered

- Usability was considered because it has broad complaint volume: usability complaints 1,079 [A3COM], severity_sum 2,563 [A3SEV], cancellations 106 [A3CAN], mean_severity 2.375348 [A3MEA]. The largest individual usability topic is Excessive and intrusive ads: complaint_count 581 [C11], severity_sum 1,238 [C12], priority_score 1,238 [C14], with cancellation intent members 53 [C15] and reviewer note `74d4c820-f915-406e-bfec-e837d2e4796f` ("ads are very annoying"). We deprioritize full usability rework this quarter because billing_support contains a higher single-issue priority and higher area severity.

- Playback contains higher-severity complaints per incident (mean_severity 3.250426 [A4MEA]) but lower aggregate area severity: complaints 587 [A4COM], severity_sum 1,908 [A4SEV], cancellations 58 [A4CAN]. Top playback topics include Playback stops/skips: complaint_count 248 [C16], severity_sum 795 [C17], priority_score 795 [C19], cancellation intent members 29 [C20], and crashes/freezes: priority_score 471 [C24]. Representative reviewer note `83b0405d-879c-4056-8aa4-5a4568065d47` ("it plays another completely random song"). Playback is important but ranks behind the billing free-tier issue by baseline priority.

- Access issues show high per-incident severity but much lower volume: access complaints 145 [A5COM], severity_sum 564 [A5SEV], cancellations 9 [A5CAN], mean_severity 3.889655 [A5MEA]. The top access issue, login failures, has complaint_count 118 [C26], severity_sum 462 [C27], priority_score 462 [C29], cancellation intent members 9 [C30], and reviewer note `19468f16-241a-4910-925f-a0d6b3526a9a` ("Can't login"). These are critical but smaller in aggregate impact than billing_support for this dataset.

Risks and limitations

- Data are self-selected Google Play reviews (May 2022–Nov 2023); not a representative customer sample. Do not infer causal or revenue effects from these aggregates.

- Labels and severity come from a small model and verifier sample; see verifier agreement in method notes. Unresolved/quarantined records excluded; coverage shows source_rows 10,000 [K01], completed_records 9,997 [K02], quarantined_records 3 [K03].

- What would change this recommendation: if downstream quantitative signals (support ticket volumes, actual cancellations, revenue telemetry) show a different ranking of impact, or if we learn billing fixes are infeasible in the quarter, shift effort to the next-highest area-level severity (usability) or high-severity playback problems.
