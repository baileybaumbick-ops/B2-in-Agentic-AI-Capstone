# Prioritize billing/support: fix free‑tier restrictions and billing communication

**Recommended area:** `billing_support`

Recommendation

Prioritize the billing/support area this quarter, focusing first on the Free tier forced shuffle and limits (billing.free_tier_restrictions) and then on billing-related user communication and support flows that surface around those constraints. Concrete first steps: (1) reduce user-facing friction and clarify which actions require Premium (skip/repeat/replay policies), (2) improve in‑app messaging and billing FAQ/appeals paths to lower stated cancellation intent, and (3) instrument support flows to measure resolution and follow‑up.

Evidence

- The single highest priority issue by our baseline is Free tier forced shuffle and limits: 47 complaints [C01], severity_sum 136 [C02] (priority_score 136) [C04], mean_severity 2.893617 [C03], with 5 members expressing cancellation intent [C05].
- Billing/support is the highest‑severity area among the four: billing_support severity_sum 154 [A2SEV], complaints 52 [A2COM], mean_severity 2.961538 [A2MEA], cancellations 6 [A2CAN].
- Representative user sentiment for the free‑tier problem: “I don't agree to to auto shuffle” (`af53cf35-c680-4ccb-b30c-cfd85bb61c57`) and “I am not like now they are so much charges to listen music” (`58511d69-67c2-4432-84d8-f3c556592f2b`).

Alternatives considered

- Playback: high severity per‑incident but lower aggregate volume than billing. Playback area has complaints 38 [A3COM] and severity_sum 135 [A3SEV] with mean_severity 3.552632 [A3MEA]. Top playback issues include stops/ skips (severity_sum 43) [C17] and connectivity errors (severity_sum 36) [C22]. Because the billing.free_tier_restrictions issue is both the top issue by priority_score and sits in the highest‑severity area, it ranks ahead of playback for next quarter effort.

- Usability: equal complaint volume to billing but lower aggregate severity. Usability has complaints 52 [A4COM] and severity_sum 125 [A4SEV]. Notable usability items include ads (severity_sum 56) [C12] and queue/playlist management (severity_sum 26) [C37]. These are important but currently have lower severity_sum than the top billing issue.

- Access: high mean severity but low volume. Access has complaints 8 [A5COM], severity_sum 33 [A5SEV], and mean_severity 4.125000 [A5MEA]. Login failures (severity_sum 28) [C27] are painful per user but are fewer reports overall than billing/support issues.

Risks and limitations

- Data coverage: source_rows 500 [K01], completed_records 500 [K02]. Reviews are self‑selected Google Play reviews (May 2022–Nov 2023) and are not a population sample; cancellation figures are expressed intent, not observed churn (method limitations).
- Labeling: topic/subtopic/severity labels derive from a small model with verifier agreement; see method notes (possible label noise).

What would change this recommendation

- If we observed actual cancellations tied to billing changes (rather than stated intent), or had revenue/segment data showing outsized premium churn linked to playback or access failures, I would reprioritize accordingly.
- If follow‑up instrumentation shows the free‑tier issue is concentrated in a small, low‑value segment, whereas playback errors are causing broader premium pain, shift effort to playback.

Bottom line: billing/support (free‑tier restrictions and clearer billing/support flows) is the highest priority by our baseline severity_sum and complaint counts; address it first and instrument for impact.
