Risks and limitations

- Data are self-selected Google Play reviews (May 2022–Nov 2023) and are not a representative customer sample; do not infer population rates, causal churn, or revenue impact. Cancellation language is stated intent only.
- Labels and severity come from a small model with verifier agreement limits (see method notes); severity labeling has uncertainty. The baseline priority uses severity_sum; if severity labeling shifts, priority ordering could change.
- Coverage metrics: source_rows 100 [K01], completed_records 100 [K02], quarantined_records 0 [K03], pending_records 0 [K04], empty_text_quarantines 0 [K05].

What would change the recommendation

- If additional data showed higher-volume or higher-severity playback or access incidents (new crash spikes or widespread login failures), their severity_sum or complaint_count could overtake billing_support. Also, if qualitative research indicates billing complaints are driven primarily by misunderstanding rather than product limits, a messaging fix might suffice instead of product changes.
